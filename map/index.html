Migration map

Where the family lived, and how it moved. Dots are places named in the records of family members who have died (larger dots, more people); lines are moves traced from one person's records. The coordinates are approximate.

  1. Latvia → Germany → Boston → Ohio → Michigan (Danenbergs). Arvīds Danenbergs: Stende (1938); refugee in Regensburg (1944–45) and the Haunstetten DP camp at Augsburg (1945–49); sailed from Bremerhaven on 28 June 1949 and landed at Boston on 7 July; farmed in Mercer County, Ohio (1950); died at Dowagiac, Michigan (1996). See Arvīds Fricis Danenbergs.
  2. Rauna → Cēsis → Germany → Boston → Toronto (Sirlaks / Smukule). Emma Sirlaka née Smukule: born in Rauna parish (1886); in Cēsis by 1914; fled to Germany in 1944 (farm work near Regensburg, then Haunstetten); crossed to Boston with her daughter's family in 1949; died in Toronto (1970). See Emma Smukule.
  3. Ireland → America → Oregon (Hall / McCabe). Susan May McCabe: born in Dublin (1887, by her US records); came to America in about 1898 (the port is not yet found); in Vancouver, Washington, with Charles Hall by 1919; in Portland from 1930 until her death (1972). Earlier versions of the family story had her landing at New York; the New York arrival found so far is another Susan McCabe. See Susan May McCabe.
  4. Texas → California → Washington (Hall / McCabe). Charles Hall: born in Texas (1891; probably Comanche, as his draft card says); registered for the draft at El Portal, Mariposa County, California (1917); trained at Camp Lewis; in Vancouver, Washington, by 1919. The identification is probable, not proven. See Charles Hall.
  5. New York → Kansas → Oregon (Estella / Alderson). Carrie May Newman (Estella's adoptive mother): born at Oak Orchard, New York (1878); in Rooks County, Kansas, as a child (1880); in Milwaukie and Portland, Oregon, from 1910. See Carrie May Newman.
