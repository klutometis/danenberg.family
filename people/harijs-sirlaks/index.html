Harijs Sirlaks 1932–2004

Peter's third cousin twice removed.

Harijs Sirlaks was born on 11 Jan 1932 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3,4, the son of Emīlis Sirlaks and Elza10. He married Dzintra11. He died on 12 Nov 2004 (according to an online family tree)7 and was buried at Jaunrauna cemetery, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia (according to an online family tree)8.
