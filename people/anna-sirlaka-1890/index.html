Anna Sirlaka 1890–1963

Peter's great-great-aunt.

Also recorded as Anna Sirlaks.

Anna Sirlaka was born on 4 Apr 1890 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3, the daughter of Pēteris Sirlaks and Marija Gibboja3,12,13. She married Tīlmanis14. She died on 29 Dec 1963 in Rīga, Vidzeme, Latvia8,9 and was buried at Rīga, Vidzeme, Latvia10,11.
