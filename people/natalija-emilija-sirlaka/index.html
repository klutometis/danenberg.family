Natālija Emīlija Sirlaka 1875–1887

Peter's great-great-aunt.

Also recorded as Natalie Emilie Sirlak (1875); Natālija Sirlaks.

Natālija Emīlija Sirlaka was born on 6 Mar 1875 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3, the daughter of Pēteris Sirlaks and Marija Gibboja2. She died on 14 Jun 18874,5 and was buried at Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia7,8.
