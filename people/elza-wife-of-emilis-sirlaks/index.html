Elza 1908–1988

Wife of Peter's second cousin three times removed Emīlis Sirlaks.

Also recorded as Elza Sirlaks; Elza (married name Sirlaks).

Elza was born in 1908 (according to an online family tree)2. She married Emīlis Sirlaks in 19306,7. She died in 1988 (according to an online family tree)3 and was buried at Jaunrauna cemetery, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia (according to an online family tree)4.
