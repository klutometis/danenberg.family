Margaret Hall 1919–1978

Peter's great-aunt.

Also recorded as Margaret H. Henry.

Margaret Hall was born on 6 Jan 1919 in Vancouver, Clark County, Washington, United States3,4, the daughter of Charles Hall and Susan May McCabe1,3,9,10,11,12. She married Robert H. Elfsten on 24 Sep 1938 in Washington, United States13. She died in Jun 1978 in Los Angeles, California, United States7,8.
