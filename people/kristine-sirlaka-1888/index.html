Kristīne Sirlaka b. 1888

Peter's great-great-aunt.

Also recorded as Kristīne Sirlaks.

Kristīne Sirlaka was born on 21 Apr 1888 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3, the daughter of Pēteris Sirlaks and Marija Gibboja2. She has died (according to an online family tree)7, but the date of death is not yet documented.
