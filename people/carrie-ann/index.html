Carrie May Newman 1878–1970

Peter's great-grandmother by adoption.

Also recorded as Carrie Ann (2015 tree); May C. Alderson (1910 census); Carrie May Schroeder (1952).

Carrie May Newman was born on 24 May 1878 in Oak Orchard, United States (according to an online family tree)1, the daughter of Edward Newman and Harriet M Brate11. She married Winter Scott Alderson on 4 Sep 1892 in Gilliam County, Oregon, United States (according to an online family tree)12. She died on 9 Aug 1970 in Lebanon, Oregon, United States1,8 and was buried at Wilhelm's Portland Memorial Mausoleum, Portland, Multnomah County, Oregon, United States1,9.
