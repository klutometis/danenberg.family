Valdis Laimdots Sirlaks 1924–1995

Peter's first cousin twice removed.

Valdis Laimdots Sirlaks was born on 28 Sep 1924 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia1,2, the son of Dāvis Sirlaks and Alma Leontīne Āboltiņa10,11. He married Valda Katlapa12. He died on 12 Oct 1995 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia7 and was buried at Jaunrauna cemetery, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia8,9.
