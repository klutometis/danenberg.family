Biruta Zeltīte Sirlaka 1919–2002

Peter's great-aunt.

Also recorded as Biruta Balka; Rūta (memoir); Biruta Sirlaks (married name Balks).

Biruta Zeltīte Sirlaka was born on 3 Jun 1919 in Cēsis, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3, the daughter of Mārtiņš Vilhelms Sirlaks and Emma Smukule13,14,15. She married Vitolds Balks between Mar 1945 and May 1945 in Regensburg, Bavaria, Germany16,17. She left Bremen-Grohn (IRO emigration camp), Germany on 22 Jan 19509. She arrived at United States in 1950 (according to an online family tree)10. She died on 30 Jun 2002 in Toronto, Ontario, Canada11,12.
