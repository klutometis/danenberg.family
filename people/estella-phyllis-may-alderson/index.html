Estella May Alderson 1916–2008

Peter's grandmother.

Also recorded as Phillis May Barns (her birth name: WA birth certificate 146-1916-005906); Phyllis (family name); Estella Hall; Phillis May Barns.

Estella May Alderson was born on 5 May 1916 in Seattle, King County, Washington, United States2,3,4,5, the daughter of John Smith and Irene Mary Barnhart, and the adopted daughter of Winter Scott Alderson and Carrie May Newman3,23,24,25. She married Charles Henry Hall on 11 May 1946 in Vancouver, Clark County, Washington, United States26,27,28. She married William S. Rowley on 30 Dec 1933 in Vancouver, Clark County, Washington, United States29. She died on 6 Jun 2008 in San Diego, California, United States5,19,20 and was buried at Fairhaven Memorial Park, Santa Ana, Santa Ana, California, United States (according to an online family tree)5.
