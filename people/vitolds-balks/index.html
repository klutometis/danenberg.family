Vitolds Balks 1921–1984

Husband of Peter's great-aunt Biruta Zeltīte Sirlaka.

Vitolds Balks was born on 16 Jun 1921 in Vecpiebalga, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia1,2. He married Biruta Zeltīte Sirlaka between Mar 1945 and May 1945 in Regensburg, Bavaria, Germany1,14. He left Bremen-Grohn (IRO emigration camp), Germany between 9 Jan 1950 and 22 Jan 195010. He arrived at New York City, New York, United States in 1950 (according to an online family tree)11. He died in Sep 198412,13.
