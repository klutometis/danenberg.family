Ilze Balka b. 1945

Peter's first cousin once removed.

Living. As for every living relative on this site, the birth date is given by year only, and no current address is shown.

Also recorded as Ilze Bērziņa; Ilze Balka (married name Augenbergs).

Ilze Balka was born in 1945 in Haunstetten DP camp, Augsburg, Augsburg, Bavaria, Germany3,4, the daughter of Vitolds Balks and Biruta Zeltīte Sirlaka10. She arrived at United States in 1950 (according to an online family tree)9.
