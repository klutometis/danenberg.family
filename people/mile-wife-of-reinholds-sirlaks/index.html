Mīle 1869–1958

Wife of Peter's first cousin 4 times removed Reinholds Sirlaks.

Also recorded as Mīle (Mill) Upīte (married name Sirlaka).

Mīle was born on 2 Feb 1869 (according to an online family tree)2. She married Reinholds Sirlaks6. She died on 21 Jun 1958 (according to an online family tree)3 and was buried at Jaunrauna cemetery, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia (according to an online family tree)4.
