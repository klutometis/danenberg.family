Christopher Alderson 1827–1907

Peter's great-great-grandfather by adoption.

Christopher Alderson was born on 11 Sep 1827 in Appleby St Michael, Westmorland, England, United Kingdom (according to an online family tree)1, the son of Christopher Alderson and Mary Veach2. He married Mary Catharine Royal on 13 Aug 1862 in Portland, Multnomah County, Oregon, United States (according to an online family tree)1. He died on 12 Oct 1907 in Albany, Oregon, United States (according to an online family tree)1 and was buried at Portland, Multnomah County, Oregon, United States (according to an online family tree)1.
