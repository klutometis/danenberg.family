Christopher Alderson 1827–1907

Peter's great-great-grandfather by adoption.

Christopher Alderson was born on 11 Sep 1827 in Bongate, Westmorland, England, United Kingdom (according to an online family tree)1, the son of Christopher Alderson and Mary Veach8. He married Mary Catharine Royal on 13 Aug 1862 in Portland, Multnomah County, Oregon, United States1,9. He died on 12 Oct 1907 in Albany, Oregon, United States1,7 and was buried at Portland, Multnomah County, Oregon, United States (according to an online family tree)1.

    Retired pioneer Methodist minister whose home was in Salem, Oregon; instantly killed on the morning of Saturday, 12 October 1907 at Albany, Linn County, Oregon, when struck by a freight train engine on the middle trestle while walking on the track to the depot after visiting his daughter (Mrs. Margaret Burkhart) in Albany, falling 30 feet to the rocks of Pennywinkle Creek; born 11 September 1827 in Yorkshire, England, immigrated to the United States in 1850, crossed the plains to Oregon in 1852, entered the Oregon Conference ministry in 1856, and married Mary C.
