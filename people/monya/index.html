Monya

Great-great-grandmother of Genevieve Elisa Symanski Nelson, wife of Peter.

Also recorded as Miriam Delabalt Phillips.

Monya.

Monya, from a Russian-Jewish family, lived in a private railroad car after the death of her husband; gambled away her fortune in Monte Carlo; and eventually resided with Elaine and Paul Fitz Gibbon in Berkeley, along with her last husband (a retired Russian sea-captain).
