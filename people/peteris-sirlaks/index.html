Pēteris Sirlaks 1847–1896

Peter's great-great-grandfather.

Pēteris Sirlaks was born on 22 Aug 1847 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3,4,5, the son of Mārcis Sirlaks and Anna Sirlaka4,17,18. He married Marija Gibboja on 22 Feb 1874 in Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia19,20,21. He died on 22 Nov 1896 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia3,9,10,11,12 and was buried at Jaunrauna cemetery, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia3,11,13,14,15.
