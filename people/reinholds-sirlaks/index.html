Reinholds Sirlaks 1862–1949

Peter's first cousin 4 times removed.

Reinholds Sirlaks was born on 20 Sep 1862 in Cēsis, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3,4, the son of Jānis Sirlaks and Līze Veidenbauma2,3. He married Mīle8. He died on 20 Apr 1949 in Tomsk oblast, Russian SFSR, USSR (deportation)5.
