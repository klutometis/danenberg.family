Dāvis Sirlaks 1886–1952

Peter's great-great-uncle.

Also recorded as David Sirlak (1886, 1912); Dāvids; Davids; Dāvis.

Dāvis Sirlaks was born on 16 Apr 1886 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3,4,5, the son of Pēteris Sirlaks and Marija Gibboja3,18,19. He married Alma Leontīne Āboltiņa about 1915 in Salaca parish, Vidzeme, Latvia20. He died on 1 Mar 1952 in Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia4,13,14 and was buried at Jaunrauna cemetery, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia15,16.

    Translation: Sirlaks, Emīls, at Kalna-Vīstuči; Sirlaks, Dāvis, at Lejas- and Jaun-Vīstuči; Strubis, Jūlijs, at Vidus-Vīstuči

Today 'Vīstuči' and 'Kalna Vīstuči' are addresses in Priekuļu pagasts near Jaunrauna cemetery (57.3374 N, 25.4608 E).
