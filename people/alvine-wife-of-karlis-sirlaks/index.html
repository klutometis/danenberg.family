Alvīne Sirlaka 1886–1968

Wife of Peter's great-great-uncle Kārlis Sirlaks.

Also recorded as Alvīne Vītiņš (married name Sirlaks).

Alvīne Sirlaka was born on 29 Nov 1886 in Cēsis, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia (according to an online family tree)2. She married Kārlis Sirlaks4. She died on 6 Aug 1968 in Rīga, Vidzeme, Latvia (according to an online family tree)3.
