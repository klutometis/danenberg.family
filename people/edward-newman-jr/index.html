Edward Newman Jr. 1875–1945

Peter's great-great-uncle by adoption.

Also recorded as Eddie Newman (1880 census); E. Newman Jr..

Edward Newman Jr. was born on 3 Jul 1875 in Yates, United States1, the son of Edward Newman and Harriet M Brate12,13. He died on 9 May 1945 in Seattle, King County, Washington, United States10 and was buried at Washington, United States11.
