  5. DANE GREASHABER Obituary (1928–2018) — Marion, OH.
    GREASHABER, DANE ALDEN age 90 of Marion, Ohio formerly of Milan, Michigan passed away Monday, April 2, 2018. He was born February 23, 1928 in Milan, Mi., to the late Walter and Pearl (Goldsmith) Greashaber. On April 24, 1948 he married Irma Ann (Burger) Greashaber, she preceded him in death on August 3, 2002.

    Her parents were DANE ALDEN GREASHABER (b. 23 February 1928 at Milan, Michigan; d. Monday 2 April 2018 at Marion, Ohio, aged 90; son of Walter and Pearl (Goldsmith) Greashaber) and IRMA ANN (BURGER) GREASHABER (married him 24 April 1948; died 3 August 2002). Dane worked for Kroger in Michigan for 21 years, then moved the family to Marion, Ohio, where he owned Dane's Fairpark Foodland for 11 years, and afterwards worked three years at Archway Cookies. In retirement he played euchre at the Marion and Pleasant Senior Centers, bowled, pitched horseshoes and followed Michigan football and basketball and the Detroit Tigers. Funeral at Marion Bible Fellowship, 5 April 2018, Deacon Kevin Kehn officiating; burial Grand Prairie Cemetery, Marion. His brothers Robert and Harold and sisters Frances Bliss and Margaret Ann Blain predeceased him. Barbara is the youngest of eight children: Dennis (Blue Springs, Missouri), Daryl (Marion), Allyson (Terry) Haycox (Marion), Derrick (Anna) (Marion), Linda (John) Sabo (North Ridgeville, Ohio), Doug (Sonya) (Marion), Dean (Lorri) (Marion), and Barbara. 14 grandchildren and 8 great-grandchildren in 2018.
