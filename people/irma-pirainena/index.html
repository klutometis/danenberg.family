    Translation: she struggled through to her mother's sister Anna in Rīga, who took her in with love. Here Irma had a daughter, Regīna, out of wedlock. …

    Starved and swollen, Irma made her way to her mother’s sister Anna (Tīlmane) in Rīga, who took her in and raised her; in Rīga Irma had a daughter Regīna out of wedlock. NB the p. 10 passage calls Irma the daughter of Anna’s sister "Erna", evidently Ernestīne.

  3. Ainas Sirlakas atmiņas ("Vīstuči"), Aina Jurciņa née Sirlaka, Priekuļi, 1998, p. 8, section "PAR PĒTERA UN MARIJAS OTRO BĒRNU ERNESTĪNI".
    Piraineņiem 4 bērni: Irma, Viktors, Aksels, Rūriks. … Dzīva paliek vienīgi Irma. Badojusies un applīsusi, atkuļas pie mātes māsas Annas Rīgā, kura mīlestībā pieņem.
    Translation: The Piraineņi had 4 children: Irma, Viktors, Aksels, Rūriks. … Only Irma survives. Starved and in rags, she struggles through to her mother’s sister Anna …

    parents: Johans Pirainens and Ernestīne née Sirlaka. Siblings Viktors, Aksels, Rūriks (died in Karelia). Raised by her aunt Anna (Tīlmane) in Rīga ("Meitas vietā audzināja māsas Ernas meitu Irmu", p. 10). Had a daughter Regīna out of wedlock ("Te Irmai bez laulības meita Regīna").
