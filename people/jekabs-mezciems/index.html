Jēkabs Mežciems 1758–1832

Peter's 5× great-grandfather.

Also recorded as Jacob 2ter Wirth (1811 revision); Jacob Jahns Sohn Wirth (1826 revision); Jacob Meschzeem Wirth (1834 revision).

Jēkabs Mežciems was born about 1758 in Priekuļi, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia1, the son of Jānis1. He died in 1832 in Priekuļi, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia3.
