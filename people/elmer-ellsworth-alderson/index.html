Elmer Ellsworth Alderson 1895–1944

Peter's great-uncle by adoption.

Also recorded as Elmer Elsworth Alderson (birth record); Elmer E. Alderson.

Elmer Ellsworth Alderson was born on 22 Oct 1895 in Portland, Multnomah County, Oregon, United States1, the son of Winter Scott Alderson and Carrie May Newman13,14. He died on 12 Mar 1944 in Portland, Multnomah County, Oregon, United States11 and was buried at Portland, Multnomah County, Oregon, United States12.
