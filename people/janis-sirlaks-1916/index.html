Jānis Sirlaks 1916–1918

Peter's first cousin twice removed.

Jānis Sirlaks was born in 1916 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia1,2, the son of Dāvis Sirlaks and Alma Leontīne Āboltiņa6,7. He died on 23 Jun 1918 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia3,4 and was buried at Jaunrauna cemetery, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia (according to an online family tree)5.
