Arvīds Fricis Danenbergs 1913–1996

Peter's grandfather.

Also recorded as Arvids F. Danenbergs; Arvids Danenburgs (1949 manifest); Arvīds Danenbergs.

Arvīds Fricis Danenbergs was born on 17 Jan 1913 in Stūrmuiža, Tukums district, Kurzeme (Courland), Latvia6,7, the son of Alberts Danenbergs and Zelma Forstmane3,78. He married Elvīra Antonija Sirlaka on 25 Jun 1941 in Litene, Vidzeme, Latvia79,80. He left Bremerhaven, Germany on 28 Jun 194947. He arrived at Boston, Massachusetts, United States on 7 Jul 194948,49,50. He applied for US citizenship on 16 Jun 1955 at Ohio, United States51. He died on 19 Feb 1996 in Dowagiac, Cass County, Michigan, United States4,52,53,54,55,56.
