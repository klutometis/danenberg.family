Ernestīne Sirlaka b. 1879

Peter's great-great-aunt.

Also recorded as Ernestīne Piraineņa; Ernestine Sirlak (godmother, 1912); Ernestīne Sirlaks.

Ernestīne Sirlaka was born on 21 Mar 1879 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3, the daughter of Pēteris Sirlaks and Marija Gibboja2,9. She married Johans Pirainens10. She has died in Siberia, USSR5,6,7, but the date of death is not yet documented.
