Alīda Āboltiņa b. 1878

Sister of Alma Leontīne Āboltiņa, wife of Peter's great-great-uncle Dāvis Sirlaks.

Alīda Āboltiņa was born in 1878 (according to an online family tree)2, the daughter of Ancis Āboltiņš and Dārta Āboltiņa4. She has died (according to an online family tree)3, but the date of death is not yet documented.
