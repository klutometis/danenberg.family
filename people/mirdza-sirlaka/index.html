Mirdza Augusta Sirlaka 1916–2010

Peter's great-aunt.

Also recorded as Mirdza Gailīte; Mirdza Augeste Gailitis; Mirdza Sirlaks.

Mirdza Augusta Sirlaka was born on 17 Jan 1916 in Vinterkalns, Cēsis, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3,4,5, the daughter of Mārtiņš Vilhelms Sirlaks and Emma Smukule15,16. She married Fricis Gailītis in May 1955 in Toronto, Ontario, Canada17. She left Bremen-Grohn (IRO emigration camp), Germany in Jan 194912. She died on 5 Jul 2010 in Windsor, Ontario, Canada13.
