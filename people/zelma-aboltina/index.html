Zelma Āboltiņa 1883–1960

Sister of Alma Leontīne Āboltiņa, wife of Peter's great-great-uncle Dāvis Sirlaks.

Also recorded as Zelma Martinsone; 'Tante' (memoir); Zelma Karolīna Āboltiņš (married name Mārtinsons).

Zelma Āboltiņa was born on 14 Oct 1883 in Salaca parish, Vidzeme, Latvia (according to an online family tree)2, the daughter of Ancis Āboltiņš and Dārta Āboltiņa7. She married Aleksandrs Martinsons8. She died about 19604,5 and was buried at Salacgrīva cemetery, on the seashore, Salacgrīva, Latvian SSR6.
