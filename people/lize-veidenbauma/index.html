Līze Veidenbauma 1836–1902

Wife of Peter's 4× great-uncle Jānis Sirlaks.

Also recorded as Līze Sirlaka; Līhze Veidenbaums / Līhze Sirlak (memoir); Līze Veidenbauma (married name Sirlaks).

Līze Veidenbauma was born on 26 Jun 1836 in Cēsis, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3. She married Jānis Sirlaks in 1855 in Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia8,9. She died on 6 Jan 1902 in Jaunrauna cemetery, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia5,6 and was buried at Jaunrauna cemetery, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia (according to an online family tree)7.
