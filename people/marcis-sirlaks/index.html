Mārcis Sirlaks b. 1821

Peter's 3× great-grandfather.

Also recorded as Mahrz Sirlakk (1847); Marz Sillak, Jahns Sohn (revisions 1834–1858).

Mārcis Sirlaks was born in 1821 (according to an online family tree)1, the son of Jānis Sirlaks and Masche2,11. He married Anna Sirlaka12. He has died (according to an online family tree)9, but the date of death is not yet documented.
