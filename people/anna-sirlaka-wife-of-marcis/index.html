Wife Marija Sirlaka 1853–1925, NOT 1920 as in the 2015 tree. Children: Natalie 1875–1887, Ernestīne 1879, Mārtiņš 1884–1942, Dāvis 1886–1952, Kristīne 1888, Anna 1890 (the memoir's aunt Anna in Rīga), Augusts 1895. 1938 court notices (Valdības Vēstnesis) mention mortgages issued by a Pēteris Sirlaks, with inheritance shares under a 16 Apr 1915 division act going to Augusts, Ernestīne and Anna Sirlaks. Very likely the same family's farm, which would place the estate division in 1915. PROBABLE SOURCE OF THE 2015 DATES: MyHeritage 'Jurcins Web Site' by Māris Jurciņš (Latvia), whose public name index spells her 'Marija Sirlaka (Ģībojs)', 'Marija Gilboy' and 'Gibboja (Ģībojs)'.
