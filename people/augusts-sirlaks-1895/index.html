Augusts Sirlaks b. 1895

Peter's great-great-uncle.

Augusts Sirlaks was born on 11 Jun 1895 in Vīstuči farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia1,2, the son of Pēteris Sirlaks and Marija Gibboja1,7. He has died (according to an online family tree)4, but the date of death is not yet documented.
