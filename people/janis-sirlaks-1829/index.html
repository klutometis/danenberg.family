Jānis Sirlaks 1829–1901

Peter's 4× great-uncle.

Also recorded as Jān Sirlak (gravestone); Sirlak Jonh (Rauna church book, per the memoir).

Jānis Sirlaks was born on 20 May 1829 in Lejas-Spāriņi farm, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia2,3,4, the son of Jānis Sirlaks and Masche15,16. He married Līze Veidenbauma in 1855 in Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia17,18. He died on 5 Mar 1901 in Jaunrauna cemetery, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia9,10 and was buried at Jaunrauna cemetery, Jaunrauna, Rauna, Cēsis district, Vidzeme, Latvia11,12.
