    The Bellevue Gazette (Bellevue, Ohio), 24 July 1957, p. 6, col. 1 ("High Cows, Herds Are Listed On June Report — Seneca Co. Tests Reported For June"): in the Seneca County Dairy Service Unit report for June 1957 (covering 33 herds under DHIA and 28 under the owner-sampler plan), A. Pladars is named among the five owners whose cows produced the most butterfat in the county during the month; among larger herds of over 20 cows, "the herd of A. Pladars averaged 40 pounds per cow" (1st place in Seneca County, ahead of Paul Wise at 39 lbs and R. L. Sigler at 38.2 lbs), and among breed leaders "the Holstein herd of A. Pladars" ranked highest in the county. Full page PDF downloaded from the Seneca East Public Library History Archives API (GetPdfFile?47008662).
