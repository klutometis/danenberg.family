Elaine de la Balt d. 1983

Great-grandmother of Genevieve Elisa Symanski Nelson, wife of Peter.

Elaine de la Balt was the daughter of de la Balt (Monya's man) and Monya4,6,7,8. She died on 6 Mar 1983 in California, United States3,4.

Elaine de la Balt, though Jewish, was raised by Catholic nuns after her mother Monya gambled away their fortune; Elaine eventually became a nurse, moved to East Bay, and there met Paul Fitz Gibbon.
